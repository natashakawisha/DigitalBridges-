Sustainability

The project prioritizes open and adaptable learning resources that can be reused and updated by schools, civil society organizations, youth hubs, and community centers.

Partnerships with local institutions, digital hubs, and educators support long-term use and scaling of the training content.

Get Involved

There are many ways to contribute to Digital Bridges Zambia:

  • Partner with us to deliver training in your community
  • Translate and localize content into local languages
  • Become a certified digital literacy facilitator
  • Support the project through funding or resources
  • Share feedback to improve our materials
