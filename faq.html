Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our digital literacy training. Can't find an answer? Reach out through the contact page.

Digital Bridges Zambia is a digital literacy initiative that develops accessible, practical, and locally relevant training content for underserved communities across Zambia. Our curriculum spans eight modules, from device basics to digital entrepreneurship.

Anyone can join. Our programmes are designed for beginners with little or no prior experience, as well as learners who want to strengthen specific skills such as online safety, mobile money, or running a business online.

A smartphone is enough to follow most of the content. For in-person community sessions, we provide shared devices so that learners without their own equipment can still participate fully.

The online learning modules are free to access once you create an account. Some facilitated community workshops may have separate arrangements depending on the partner organisation hosting them.

Click Sign Up in the navigation bar, enter your name, email, and a password of at least six characters, then submit the form. You will be taken straight to your dashboard where you can track your progress.

Most modules take between two and three hours to complete. You can learn at your own pace — your progress is saved, so you can stop and continue whenever it suits you.

Yes. Once you mark a module as completed on your dashboard, it counts towards your overall progress, and learners who complete the full curriculum are eligible for a completion certificate.

For security, after three failed sign-in attempts the login form locks for 30 seconds. If you cannot remember your password, contact our support team through the contact page and we will help you regain access.

Absolutely. The entire platform is mobile-friendly, so you can read lessons, watch content, and track your progress from any phone or tablet with an internet connection.

Use the form on our contact page and choose the subject that best matches your question. Our team responds by email, usually within one to two working days.
